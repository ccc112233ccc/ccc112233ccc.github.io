# About

Hi, I’m burger. This is where I take complex systems apart, inspect the layers,
and put them back together with evidence.

The main threads are:

- distributed training and inference for large models;
- collective communication, network topology, and performance analysis;
- discrete-event simulation, system modeling, and validation;
- papers and implementations worth understanding from first principles.

Posts may be written in English or Chinese. The goal is not to paraphrase an
abstract, but to answer three questions:

- What problem did the work actually solve?
- Why should the method work, and is the evidence convincing?
- How does it connect to the systems and papers around it?

Notes evolve as my understanding changes. Corrections and discussions are
welcome on GitHub.
